DataVisualization_Diary

What is the main story or message your visualizations or article communicate?

- The visualizations show that machine learning models, especially XGBoost, can effectively 
rank borrowers by default risk, even in an imbalanced dataset. The results highlight that a 
small portion of high-risk borrowers accounts for a large share of defaults, making risk segmentation 
highly valuable for decision-making. Overall, the dashboard demonstrates how model performance, 
threshold selection, and feature patterns work together to support better lending strategies.


Sketch of you dashboard or outline of your article sections

- The dashboard is organized into several sections: a model leaderboard comparing performance metrics, 
a threshold explorer showing classification trade-offs, a ROC/PR/calibration panel evaluating model quality, 
a decile analysis highlighting risk segmentation, and a single-loan explorer for individual predictions. 
Each section builds on the previous one, moving from overall model comparison to detailed insights and practical
decision support.
