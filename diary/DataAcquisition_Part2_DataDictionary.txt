# List variables you plan to use
## Brief description of what each variable represents and other data dictionary attributes

-Target variable
 loan_status: Final status of the loan  (commonly: Fully Paid, Charged Off, Default, Late)

-Borrower Demographics & Financial Capacity
 annual_inc: Borrower’s self-reported annual income     (Numeric)
 emp_length: Length of borrower’s employment	(Categorical) 
 home_ownership: Home ownership status  ((Categorical (RENT, OWN, MORTGAGE)))
 verification_status: Whether income was verified   (Categorical (Verified / Not Verified))

-Credit History & Creditworthiness
 fico_range_low: Lower bound of borrower’s FICO score range     (Numeric)
 fico_range_high: Upper bound of borrower’s FICO score range    (Numeric)
 earliest_cr_line: Date of borrower’s earliest credit line  (Used to derive credit history length)
 open_acc: Number of open credit lines	(Numeric)
 total_acc: Total number of credit lines	(Numeric)
 delinq_2yrs: Number of delinquencies in past 2 years	(Numeric)
 pub_rec: Number of derogatory public records	(Numeric)

-Debt Burden & Repayment Capacity
 dti: Debt-to-income ratio	(Key risk indicator)
 revol_bal: Total revolving credit balance	(Numeric)
 revol_util: Revolving credit utilization rate (%)	(Numeric, may need cleaning)

-Loan Characteristics (Known at Origination)
 loan_amnt: Amount of loan funded	(Numeric)
 term: Loan repayment term	    (Categorical (36 or 60 months))
 int_rate: Interest rate on the loan	(Numeric)
 installment: Monthly payment amount	(Derived from loan terms)
 purpose: Purpose of the loan   (Categorical (e.g., debt consolidation))
 grade: LendingClub assigned loan grade	    (Ordinal (A–G))
 sub_grade: More granular loan grade	(Ordinal (e.g., A1–G5))

-Temporal / Control Variables
 issue_d: Month the loan was issued	(Used for time-based train/test split)
 addr_state: Borrower’s state	(Optional; may raise fairness concerns)
