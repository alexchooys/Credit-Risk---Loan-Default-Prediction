DataExploration Part2

What issues have you identified (duplicates, missingness, standardization, noise/outliers, etc.)?
For each issue: where it appears (table/column), how big it is (counts/percent), and what can it mean.


Issue 1: Extensive Missingness in Non-Core Columns

    Where:
     member_id (100% missing)
     Hardship-related fields (hardship_reason, hardship_type, etc. >99% missing)
     next_pymnt_d (~99.8% missing)

    Magnitude:
     Entire columns or near-entire columns missing

    Meaning:
     These fields only apply to a small subset of borrowers
     Not suitable for general default prediction
     Should be excluded from modeling and DB core tables

Issue 2: Moderate Missingness in Key Predictors

    Where:
     annual_inc
     emp_length
     revol_util

    Magnitude:
     Non-trivial but far from majority

    Meaning:
     Missingness may be informative (e.g., unverified or unstable borrowers)
     Requires explicit handling (imputation or missing indicators)


Issue 3: High Cardinality & Unstructured Text

    Where:
     emp_title
     desc
     title

    Magnitude:
     Thousands of unique values
     Many rare categories

    Meaning:
     Not directly usable in structured models
     Requires NLP, grouping, or exclusion

Issue 4: Outliers & Skewness

    Where:
     annual_inc
     dti
     revol_bal

    Magnitude:
     Small proportion of extreme values

    Meaning:
     Can distort linear models
     Often caused by self-reported data or data entry quirks

Issue 5: Multicollinearity

    Where:
     fico_range_low vs fico_range_high
     grade, sub_grade, int_rate

    Magnitude:
     Structural and systematic

    Meaning:   
     Redundant information
     Affects interpretability and coefficient stability

Issue 6: Duplicate / Identifier Noise

    Where:
     id and loan_id-like fields
     member_id (entirely missing)

    Magnitude:
     Structural

    Meaning:
     IDs should not be used as predictors
     Need to clearly define primary keys


What questions would you ask your stakeholders to resolve these issues in the future?

    About Target Definition
     Which loan outcomes are officially considered “default” for risk reporting?
     Should serious delinquencies be modeled separately from final defaults?

    About Missing Data
     Why are income and employment missing for some borrowers?
     Should missing values be treated as risk signals?

    About Outliers
     Are income and DTI values capped internally?
     Are extremely high values audited or verified?

    About Institutional Variables
     Should internal grades be used for prediction or only for benchmarking?
     Is the goal to replicate or improve upon current underwriting?

    About Unstructured Data
     Is job title information standardized internally?
     Are free-text descriptions used in real underwriting decisions?

    About Fairness & Compliance
     Is geographic information permitted in decision-making?
     Are there regulatory constraints on feature usage?
