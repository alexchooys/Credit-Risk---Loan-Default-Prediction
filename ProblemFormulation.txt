Credit Risk & Loan Default Prediction

What is the central question? 
-Can we accurately predict whether a loan applicant is likely to default on a loan based on their financial and credit information? 
-Predicting whether a borrower will failure to fulfill an obligation on a loan or not.

Why is it important in context?
-Loan defaults cause significant financial losses for banks and lenders.
-Reducing bad loans, making better loan approval decisions, setting appropriate interest rates, and complying with risk management regulations.

What would “success” look like?
-A success would mean lower default rates without rejecting too many good customers.


What variables are essential, and why?
-Credit score, income, debt-to-income ratio, payment history, and loan amount & loan term.
-These variables are essential because they directly influence a borrower’s capacity and willingness to repay, which are core concepts in credit risk analysis.

Who will use the results?
-Loan officers, risk analysts, bank management, and compliance teams.


Is this realistic given constraints?
-Yes, I think it is realistic. It closely mirrors real-world banking use cases. Public datasets are available in Kaggle and UCI.
