Data acquisition (sources and relevance)

Where the data would come from
-Source: Kaggle dataset "Lending Club Loan Data" by wordsforthewise
-https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wordsforthewise/lending-club/data?select=accepted_2007_to_2018Q4.csv.gz

How it would be collected
-LendingClub collects borrower information during the loan application process, such as income, employment status, and credit history.
-Loan performance data is collected over the life of the loan through scheduled repayment transactions.
-Credit-related variables (e.g., FICO score ranges) are sourced from credit bureaus via regulated data-sharing agreements.

Known or expected limitations
-The model is trained on accepted loans, which introduces sample selection bias. However, since loan outcomes are only observable for 
approved borrowers, this approach is standard in industry. The model is intended to assess default risk post-approval rather than to
determine loan eligibility.
-Selection bias: Only approved loans are included, meaning repayment outcomes for rejected applicants are not observed.
-Self-reported information: Variables such as income may contain inaccuracies.
-Limited feature scope: Public data does not include full transaction histories or external economic indicators used by real banks.
